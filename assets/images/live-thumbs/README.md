# Channel logo sources

The transparent broadcaster PNG marks in this directory are primarily sourced from the
[tv-logo/tv-logos](https://github.com/tv-logo/tv-logos) catalog at commit
`d32e347bb7c4c640dceec23957802ad9182f58a6` (2 May 2026). The matching mobile
assets are copied from the same files.

Canal 11's mark is sourced from its current official site. Sport TV 7 uses the
channel launch artwork published in August 2024; Sport TV's current site confirms
that the channel remains part of its lineup. The LALIGA TV mark replaces the old
Viaplay Sports La Liga presentation because Viaplay states that its former UK sports
subscriptions moved to Premier Sports.

The marks remain the trademarks of their respective broadcasters and are bundled
only to identify the corresponding channels. The source catalog describes the
assets as Creative Commons ShareAlike and requests attribution for redistribution.
